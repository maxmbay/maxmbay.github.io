Public projects

audience predictor

The aim is to take a screenplay nobody has filmed yet and answer two questions: who the film is for, and whether the pages are any good.

Who it is for comes from placing the script near films that millions of people have already rated, which yields similar titles and a sense of whether audiences would split. Whether the pages are any good comes from reading the script scene by scene and returning a pass, consider, or recommend. Both answers use only the script, because cast, ads, reviews, and prizes do not exist yet.

catalog value

The aim is to estimate how much a film adds to a catalog by covering viewers that catalog does not already serve.

People like different things, so a catalog’s value is how much of that spread it covers. The model learns those tastes and each title’s place among them, then measures how coverage changes if the title is added or removed. The result is a range for that coverage.

cellresponse

The aim is to find which drug moves the largest share of a cancer cell line into a chosen state, such as stopping division while a general stress response stays off.

Cells in the same dish respond unevenly, so an average hides the result that matters. The project predicts the whole group of cells after treatment, using the untreated cells, the drug’s chemical structure, and the dose, and then counts how many land in that state. phoenix tracker

I’m a big fan of Oliver Widger, who is sailing the boat, and this is a fan site that tells his story.

The map, the weather charts, and the posts all follow one selected hour, so the story stays tied to where he was. Wind and wave height are reconstructed at the reported position and kept separate from anything he said. It is unofficial, and it is not affiliated with him, the voyage, or PredictWind.
